## Why Program?

When LLMs are so easy to access today, why take time to learn how to program? We can ask an agent to generate entire apps with ease. Do we really need to peek under the hood at the mysteries of generated code?

Maybe someday our efforts to program will be irrelevant, but I suspect that agentic development will have the same impact on programming as calculators had on Mathematics. Who today would claim that calculators are not a useful *tool* for studying Mathematics? Yet, I would claim that it is essential to unpack the details of the operations the calculator performs so that you have a firm understanding to build more advanced concepts.

So it is with programming. Let's peek under the hood and unpack the ideas so central to our modern world!

## What Language?

Any discussion on programming must be grounded in an actual language to program. [Python](https://www.python.org/) seems to be the de facto language to teach programming because of its ease of use. While I agree that it’s a great language, I think it’s better to start your journey in [Rust](https://rust-lang.org/). There are many reasons Rust is a great language of choice for learning to program. Rust’s strictness forces you to deal with how information is defined and flows through the system. Higher level languages like Python hide these details from you.
